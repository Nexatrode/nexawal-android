NexaWal is a free and open-source (MIT) Monero wallet for Android.

• Create or import a single wallet; seed stays on your device
• Sync against a Monero daemon you choose (default https://rpc.nexatrode.com)
• Clearnet, I2P, or hybrid node routing
• Receive with QR / payment URI and subaddresses
• Send with fee preview
• Optional device biometrics / screen lock for unlock and send
• Optional fiat estimates (off by default)

No Google Play services, no ML Kit, no third-party analytics.

Source: https://github.com/Nexatrode/nexawal-android
Privacy: https://nexatrode.com/privacy/nexawal/
Reproducible builds: see docs/REPRODUCIBLE_BUILD.md in the repository.

Unaudited software. You are responsible for backups and funds. A remote node can see your IP and sync queries.
